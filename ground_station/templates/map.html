LoRa encryption

AES encrypt telemetry

Preflight only: sync the key to the Pi while it is still on the ground (Wi‑Fi on). The key is saved to lora_crypto.json on the Pi — no Wi‑Fi needed in flight. Wi‑Fi turns off at launch; LoRa keeps using the stored key until landing. Confirm Key on Pi: Matched before release. Encryption does not lower RF power; packets are ~75 bytes longer, adding ~0.4–0.5 s air time per TX on SF12 — fine at economy 55 s, still under 10% duty.

LoRa settings

Channel (CH) — E22 frequency slot. Roughly 410.125 + CH MHz (CH24 ≈ 434.1 MHz on our hardware). Pi and ground must use the same CH.

Air 3 / 4 / 5 — LoRa mode presets on the E22 (not literal kbps). On our modules: air 3 = SF12 + 125 kHz BW (best range, flight default); air 4 = SF12 + 125 kHz; air 5 = SF12 + 250 kHz (faster, shorter range). Both ends must match.

Flight default: CH24 + air 3 @ 10 dBm on the HAB.

LoRa downlink
